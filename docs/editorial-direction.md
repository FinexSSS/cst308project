# Editorial direction

Provisional. Confirmed once the writings are supplied.

## Chosen system (user-selected)

- Palette: bone #F2F0E6 ground, monsoon green #2F4436, moss #8C9A5B accent, ink #1A1A14.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond display (roman + italic) with Karla for body and labels.
- Layout: asymmetric magazine spread — 7/5 split, oversized masthead, left-ruled secondary
  column, dark green archive block with oversized ampersand, printed contents index.
- Motion: scroll-triggered stagger on blocks, slow image scale on hover, grayscale-to-colour
  reveals, animated filter indicator, drawing hairlines, a slow label marquee. All disabled under
  prefers-reduced-motion.

## Considered and rejected

Warm-paper serif quarterly (too quiet), night-river dark palette, clay/indigo, kinetic anthology
with floating blocks, framed-folio bordered spread.

## Unresolved

Real titles and text, dates, author identity, publication name, language/script mix, whether the
archive needs tags or issues, and whether a five-second ambient video is wanted.
